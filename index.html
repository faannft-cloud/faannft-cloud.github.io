Metric definitions & calculation notes
CSAT
Satisfied responses ÷ valid survey responses. Ratings of 4 or 5 on a five-point scale count as satisfied.
Average handling time
Total handling seconds ÷ handled contacts, shown in minutes. Handling includes active work, hold time, and after-contact work.
First-response SLA
Eligible assigned contacts with a first response within 24 hours ÷ all eligible assigned contacts. This is an illustrative asynchronous service workflow.
Audit quality
Audit points earned ÷ total available audit points. Different numbers of audits contribute different weights.

Each selected metric uses the sum of its numerators divided by the sum of its denominators. Weekly percentages are never averaged to produce the period total. Values are rounded only for display. W1–W8 are fixed relative weeks; “last” refers to W8, not today's date. A14 has no survey or audit observations in W8, so those metrics show “No data”. No performance targets are assumed.

The investigation / A07 · Week 4

What would I
look at next?

A fixed coaching example, separate from the dashboard filters. The numbers show a change; they do not tell us its cause.

  1. 01

    Observe the pattern

    Compare A07's week 3 and week 4 customer feedback and handling time, including the number of survey responses behind each percentage.

  2. 02

    Form a hypothesis

    A change in case complexity, an unclear procedure, or a tool issue could contribute to the pattern. A knowledge gap is one possibility, not a conclusion about the agent.

  3. 03

    Check the cases behind the numbers

    Review the negative survey comments and a sample of handled cases. Compare case types and escalation paths, check for policy or system changes, and ask the agent where work became difficult. These are proposed checks; the demo contains no underlying customer cases.

  4. 04

    Agree on a specific action

    If the review confirms a procedure gap, walk through a relevant case together, clarify the decision steps, and record the guidance in a short knowledge note. Set an owner and a follow-up date. A system issue would need a different action.

  5. 05

    Follow up without overstating the result

    Review comparable cases and the next weeks of feedback, quality, and handling time together. A later change is a reason to investigate further, not proof that coaching caused an improvement. No intervention was performed in this simulation.
